# https://www.shanfoods.com/product/recipe-mixes/curry/chicken-masala/
COOKING | RECIPE | As per 50 gram | Ingredient Required | Chicken | 1 kg/2.2 lbs | small portions | Onions | 3 medium / 300g | finely sliced or (Fried Onion 60g / ½ cup) | Plain Yogurt | 1 ½ cups / 300g | whipped | Ginger Julienne | 2 tablespoons | Oil / Ghee | 1 cup / 175 ml | Shan Chicken Masala Mix | 1 packet | mix in ½ cup water | Steps of Cooking | Heat oil, fry onions until light golden, remove  and crush. | In the same oil add chicken, yogurt and Shan Chicken Masala Mix. Cover and cook on low heat for 20-30 minutes. | Add julienne ginger and ½ -2 cups water for gravy. Cover and cook on low heat for 5 minutes. | Stir-in crushed onions. Cover and cook on low heat for 5 minutes.

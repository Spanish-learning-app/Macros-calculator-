# The Perfect Sweet Lassi Recipe
https://www.teaforturmeric.com/lassi/
yield: ["2","2 lassis or 4 cups (32 oz) total"]
- 1 1/2 cup plain, whole-milk yogurt
- 4 large/8 small  ice cubes
- 3/4-1 cup whole milk (depending on how thick or thin you prefer it)
- 4 tbsp + 2 tsp  cane sugar (or to taste - See Note 1)
- 3 tbsp heavy whipping cream (or sub half and half)
1. Add all ingredients to a blender. Blend on high speed until smooth and no longer icy (~1 minute). It&#39;ll naturally become frothy on top.
2. Taste and add sugar for sweetness, ice for coolness, or 1-2 tbsp of yogurt for tartness. Serve immediately or refrigerate, covered, for 1-2 days.

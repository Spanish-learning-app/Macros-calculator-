# Instant Pot Pakistani Chana Dal
https://www.teaforturmeric.com/instant-pot-pakistani-chana-dal/
yield: ["4","4 servings"]
- 3/4 cup chana dal  (sub yellow split chickpeas - rinsed several times and then drained (See Note 1))
- 2 cups water
- 1  small to medium tomato (finely chopped)
- 1 tsp kosher salt (or to taste)
- 1/2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/4 tsp cumin powder
- 1/4 tsp red chili powder (or more to taste)
- 1/8 tsp ground black pepper
- 2 tbsp ghee (or sub butter or oil)
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 small yellow onion (or ½ medium onion) ( thinly sliced)
- 3  garlic cloves (crushed or finely chopped)
- 1 small green chili pepper (or sub 4-5 whole red chili pepper) (chopped)
- 1/4 tsp garam masala
- 1/4 tsp freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 1/2 inch ginger (julienned)
- 2 tbsp cilantro leaves (chopped)

# Puri Chole in Under 30 Minutes
https://www.flourandspiceblog.com/puri-chole-nashta-recipe/
yield: ["4"]
- 1  onion (100g)
- 3/4 tsp ginger (grated or 1 tsp paste)
- 3/4 tsp garlic (crushed or 1 tsp paste)
- 1  can chickpeas (19 oz)
- 1  teabag
- 3/4 tsp kalonji/ nigella seeds
- 3/4 tsp mustard seeds
- 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp salt (unless your chickpeas are salty, then adjust at the end)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric /haldi
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1/2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp saunf/aniseed  ((roughly crushed))
1. Saute the onion in a little oil on medium heat until it&#039;s lightly golden
2. Add, ginger and garlic and saute for 30 seconds
3. Now add all the remaining ingredients except the teabag and mix well
4. Pour a cup and a half of hot water into the pot, bring to boil and simmer for 15 minutes
5. Put the teabag into the pot, mix, and simmer until the chickpeas are tender - varies by brand, but this takes another 10 minutes for me
6. Take the teabag out
7. Lightly mash some of the chickpeas with a spoon and stir, add water to preferred consistency and adjust seasoning to preference.

# Pakora Mix & How to Make Pakoras
https://www.flourandspiceblog.com/homemade-pakora-mix/
yield: undefined
- 4 cups  besan
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp chilli flakes
- 1  tbsp chilli powder
- 1 tbsp coriander powder
- 1 tbsp cumin seeds
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp  ajwain (optional)
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
1. Combine all the dry ingredients in a clean air tight jar, they will easily last two weeks or more.
2. To make pakoras - take out some of the dry mix.For plain pakoras put 3/4 the amount of water to the dry mix and fry on medium heat till golden and for vegetable ones start with half a cup water to 1 cup mix and adjust if needed.
3. Tip: Draining your pakoras on a wire rack (versus paper towel) will lead to a crispier pakora!

# Bhindi ki Sabzi
https://www.flourandspiceblog.com/bhindi-ki-sabzi-or-spicy-pakistani-style-okra/
yield: undefined
- 1 lb Okra
- 3-4  dried whole red chillies
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- ½-3/4  red chilli flakes
- 3/4-1  salt
- Oil for cooking (a little less than ¼ cup)
1. Slice the okra into 1/2 cm coins.
2. Heat oil in a saucepan, when it shimmers add the cumin seeds and dried whole red chillies
3. Add the the salt and the chilli flakes to the oil, then add the okra and stir deftly to coat well.
4. Cook uncovered on medium heat, stirring it every 5-6 minutes, the bhindi will darken, and cook through in 15-18 minutes for soft tender bhindi.
5. For a darker bhindi with more of a chew turn the heat up and saute a few more minutes until it&#39;s to your desired doneness. Adjust seasoning.

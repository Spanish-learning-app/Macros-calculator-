# Easy Pista Kulfi Recipe – No Cook, 6 Ingredients
https://www.flourandspiceblog.com/easy-pista-kulfi-recipe/
yield: ["8","8 kulfis, OR 12 servings in a tray"]
- 3 slices white bread ((remove crust for a smoother kulfi, but yummy with too))
- 50 g slivered pistachios (1/3 cup)
- 300 ml condensed milk
- 350 ml evaporated milk
- 250 ml  full fat cream (1 cup)
- 1/4 tsp salt
1. Blend until smooth .
2. Transfer to your container of choice and freeze till firm. Keeps well for up to 6 months.
3. Tip: If using a loaf tin or if you want to serve it pre sliced then lining the dish with plastic wrap makes for easier removal.

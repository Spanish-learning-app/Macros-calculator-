COOKING | RECIPE | As per  gram | Ingredient Required | Ground/Minced Meat with fats | 1kg | minced twice | Garlic Paste | 2 tbsp | Onion | 1 small | finely diced (squeeze to remove liquid) | Green Chilies | 1-2 tbsp | coarsely ground | Egg whites | 1 | Melted Better | 2-3 tablespoons | Shan Tikka Seekh Kabab Mix | 1 packet | Steps of Cooking | Add onion, garlic paste, green chilies, egg and Shan Tikka Seekh Kabab Mix to minced meat and set aside for 2-3 hours. | Add butter and knead well. Make small meat balls. | Skewer through each meat ball. Flatten minced meat around the skewer with wet hands to make thin Kababs. | Place on low heat of coal fire until light brown. Turn the skewers periodically. (Do not over brown the Seekh Kababs). Serving Suggestions: Serve with Shan Tamarind / BBQ / Green Chutney and onion salad.

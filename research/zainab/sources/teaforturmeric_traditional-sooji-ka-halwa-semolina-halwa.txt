# Sooji ka Halwa (Semolina Halwa) - 20 Minute Recipe!
https://www.teaforturmeric.com/traditional-sooji-ka-halwa-semolina-halwa/
yield: ["4","4 servings"]
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter or ghee (See Note 1)
- 1 cup fine semolina (sooji)
- 2 1/2 cups water  (See Note 2)
- 3/4 cup raw cane sugar or turbinado (demerara) sugar (See Note 3)
- 4-5  cardamom pods (slightly cracked open)
- pinch  saffron strands (lightly crushed between your fingers, optional)
- 1/2 tsp rose water
- 1-2 tbsp slivered almonds (for garnish (optional))
1. Heat a large, nonstick skillet over medium-low heat. Add the butter or ghee and once it melts, immediately add the semolina (sooji).
2. Cook the semolina, stirring frequently, until it roasts evenly and turns deep golden in color (~12-14 min). Depending on your stovetop, you may need to raise the heat to help it come to the roasting point. Then lower the heat if needed. Meanwhile, start with the sugar syrup.
3. In a small to medium saucepan, bring the water, sugar, cardamom pods, and saffron (if using) to a simmer. Stir until the sugar is dissolved, then remove from heat. Add the rose water, if using.
4. Pour the sugar syrup carefully into the browned semolina. Continue to stir for 3-4 minutes, until the mixture thickens and the butter starts to separate from the semolina. Sometimes I like to add a tablespoon of ghee at this point to give it a nice finish.
5. Garnish with slivered almonds. Serve hot, warm, or at room temperature. If you&#39;re not serving immediately, store in an airtight container to prevent it from drying out.

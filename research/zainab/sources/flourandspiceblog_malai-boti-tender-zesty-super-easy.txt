# Malai Boti
https://www.flourandspiceblog.com/malai-boti-tender-zesty-super-easy/
yield: ["4","4 8-inch skewers"]
- 400 g chicken breast
- 3 tbsp plain yoghurt
- 3 tbsp cream (unsweetened)
- 2 tsp green chili paste  ((halve for mild))
- 1 tsp roasted cumin powder
- 1  tsp roasted coriander powder
- 1.5 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 3/4-1 tsp salt
- 1 1/4 tsp garlic paste
- 1 1/4 tsp ginger paste
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
1. Cut your chicken breast into even 3/4-1 inch pieces - I got roughly 22 pieces from my 2 medium sized chicken breasts
2. Combine the marinade ingredients with the chicken cubes and set aside to rest for a minimum of four hours and up to two days
3. Remove the chicken from the fridge and let it come to room temperature
4. If using wooden skewers soak them in water for 30-minutes
5. Stovetop: medium high heat, 3 minutes per each side, I like to get all four for a cube of chicken
6. Air Fryer: 6 minutes per side at 390F
7. Oven:  Cover the exposed ends of wooden skewers with foil (to be safe) and bake on a greased tray lined with foil for 18-20 minutes at 375F, flipping halfway. Broil for additional color - but watch this carefully!
8. Grill: 6-8 minutes per side, but please bear in mind timings vary with grilling so keep a close eye on it!

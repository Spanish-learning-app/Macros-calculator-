# Chicken Dal Gosht and 25+ Top Rated Pakistani Chicken Recipes!
https://www.flourandspiceblog.com/dal-chicken-or-chicken-dal-gosht-in-the-instant-pot/
yield: ["6"]
- 1 lb boneless chicken cut into 2 inch chunks, or 1.5 lb bone in chicken
- 1 onion diced (3/4 cup)
- 1 tsp garlic paste (heaped)
- 1 tsp ginger paste (heaped)
- 1-2  tomatoes (diced) (1 for a milder tomato flavour (my preference) or 2 for more robust masala )
- 1 + 1/2 tsp salt ((1/2 to be added with the daal))
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp cayenne / chili powder
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp masoor ki daal
- 1 cup chanay ki daal
- 1 tbsp kasuri methi
- 1 tsp garam masala (powder)
- 2 tsp chaat masala (optional)
- squeeze of lemon juice
- 2-3 green chilies
- 1/2  onion sliced thinly
- 3-4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 3-4  whole dried red chilies
- 3/4 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 sprig curry leaves (optional)
1. Put the IP on Saute mode on medium, pour in about 1/4 cup of oil, then saute onions till the edges turn golden
2. Add the ginger + garlic and saute for a minute
3. Now add the tomatoes and all the spices (but only 1 tsp of salt) along with a splash of water, mix well and saute until the oil rises above the masala (bhunn)
4. Then add the chicken pieces, mix well and saute for 3-4 minutes until the color changes and the chicken absorbs some of that great masala
5. Add in the daals, 1/2 tsp salt, a green chili or two if you want it spicy and 3 cups water and set it to manual for 10 minutes,
6. Let it be for ten minutes then quick release.
7. Add kasuri methi, garam masala, chaat masala if using, a squeeze of lemon juice and check for seasoning. Adjust to taste.
8. Heat oil in a small frying pan, about 1/4 cup and once it&#039;s shimmering add all the baghaar ingredients and keep on medium flame till the onions are a beautiful golden brown
9. Pour over your daal gosht and enjoy with some hot fluffy rice!

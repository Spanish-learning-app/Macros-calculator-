# How to Make Doodh Patti
https://www.flourandspiceblog.com/how-to-make-doodh-patti/
yield: ["1"]
- 1/2  tbsp tea leaves  (a spoonful of tea leaves) (or 1 teabag )
- 2 cups milk  (1 1/3 of a mug) ((or half water, half milk))
- 3  cardamom pods (lightly crushed)
- v small piece of cinnamon (a cm)
- sugar to taste
1. Bring the milk, cardamom, and cinnamon to a boil. Tip: leave a heat proof spoon horizontally across the pot to curtail boiling over.
2. Once the milk has come to a boil aerate with a ladle a few times to create a beautiful creamy froth.
3. Add your tea leaves, bring to a boil
4. Aerate a few times with a ladle and keep it at an aggressive simmer for at least 5 minutes. Your mixture should have reduced by at least a third from where you started.
5. Strain and serve!

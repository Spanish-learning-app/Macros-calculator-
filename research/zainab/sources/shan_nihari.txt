COOKING | RECIPE | As per 60 gram | Ingredient Required | Meat | 1kg / 2.2lbs | 4-6 large portions | Bones | 2kg / 4.4lbs | knuckle & marrow | Onions | 1 medium | finely sliced | Flour | 1 cup / 100g | dissolve in 2 cups water | Cooking Oil | 1 cup /175 ml | Shan Nihari Mix | 1 packet | mix in ½ cup water | Steps of Cooking | Heat ½ cup oil, add meat and Shan Nihari Mix. Stir fry until oil separates from masala. | Add 18 cups / 3 ½ liters water and bones. Stir and bring to boil. Cover and cook undisturbed on low heat (for: Beef 6 hours, Goat / Lamb 4 hours, Chicken 3 hours). | Remove bones, collect marrow and discard bones. Add marrow to gravy . | Gradually add dissolved flour in the gravy. Stir and mix. Bring to boil and cook for 15 minutes. Stir occasionally. | Heat remaining oil and add sliced onion. Stir fry until golden and pour into Nihari. Cover and simmer for 10 minutes on low heat. | Remove excessive oil before serving, if desired

## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Frying pan (24–28 cm)
- Small pot (for 2 eggs)
- Salad spinner or a clean tea towel
- Jar or bowl for the dressing
- 2 lunch boxes and 2 small lidded pots (about 100 ml)

**Before you start**

- If the chicken breast (260 g) is thicker than 1.5 cm, cut it through the middle into 2 thin fillets.
- Peel 2 garlic cloves. Grate 1 (3 g) for the chicken and a small piece (2 g) for the dressing.
- Cut the 2 romaine hearts (400 g) into strips 2 cm wide. Halve the cherry tomatoes (200 g).

**Steps**

1. Put 2 eggs in the small pot, cover with cold water and bring to the boil. Turn down so it bubbles gently and cook 10 minutes. *(high heat · 10 min)*
2. While the eggs cook, rub the chicken with the 3 g grated garlic, a heaped ¼ tsp salt (2 g) and black pepper (1 g). *(no heat)*
3. Still while the eggs cook, heat 1½ tsp olive oil (6 g) in the frying pan for 1 minute. *(medium-high heat · 1 min)*
4. Add the chicken. Cook 3–4 minutes without moving it, until golden underneath. *(medium-high heat · 4 min)*
5. Turn it over and cook 3–4 minutes more. *(medium-high heat · 4 min)*
6. Check: cut the chicken at its thickest point. White all through with clear juices. Pink? 1–2 minutes more a side. Then let it cool on a plate. *(no heat)*
7. Pour the hot water off the eggs, cover them with cold water and leave 5 minutes. Peel and halve them: the yolks should be firm. *(no heat · 5 min)*
8. Wash the lettuce in cold water and dry it very well in the salad spinner or by rolling it in a clean tea towel. Wet leaves go soggy. *(no heat)*
9. Make the dressing: mix 120 g Greek yogurt (about 6 tbsp), 2 tsp Dijon mustard (10 g), 1 tbsp lemon juice (16 g), 1 tbsp + 1 tsp olive oil (18 g), the 2 g grated garlic, black pepper (1 g) and a pinch of salt (1 g). Pour it into the 2 pots. *(no heat)*
10. Slice the chicken. Fill each box: half the lettuce, half the tomatoes, half the chicken slices and 1 egg in halves. The dressing pot and the bag of picos (40 g per box) go next to the box, not on the salad. *(no heat)*
11. Later, at lunch: pour the dressing over, close the lid and shake, then break the picos over the top. *(no heat)*

**Done when:** The chicken is white all through with clear juices (75 °C); the egg yolks are firm.

**Box it up:** Cool the chicken for no more than 1 hour, then box everything with the dressing pots. Both boxes go in the fridge: eat them within 2 days (by Tuesday if you make them on Sunday). Do not freeze. Make 2 boxes on Sunday night and 2 more on Tuesday night. The picos stay in their sealed bag in the cupboard. Carry in an insulated bag with an ice pack.

**Reheat:** Not reheated: eat cold within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour on a day above 32 °C).

**Tips**

- The dressing uses yogurt, mustard and lemon: no anchovy, no cheese, so nothing to check for rennet.

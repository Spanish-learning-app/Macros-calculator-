## Method

> TODO (brief s3-method): this sheet still has the short cook's method below; the beginner method (equipment, before you start, steps with heat and time, done when, box it up, reheat) is not written yet, so the app shows no method for it.

1. Heat the oil, crackle the cumin seeds, then soften the onion 8–10 min. Add garlic and ginger for 1 min.
2. Add the mince and break it up; brown on high heat 8–10 min until the liquid has gone.
3. Add tomato, green chilli, yogurt, ground spices and salt. Cover and cook 15 min on low (add a splash of water only if it sticks), then uncover and dry it out.
4. Add the frozen peas with a splash of water, cover 5 min, then cook uncovered until dry: a box keema should have no loose gravy. Finish with black pepper, garam masala, coriander and lemon.
5. Cook the basmati by absorption (about 1 : 1.5 rice to water by volume, 12 min + 5 min rest).
6. Cool within 1 h, then pack ¼ of the rice and ¼ of the keema per box.

Use-by of a packed box = cooked date + the shortest fridge life in it = **1 day**. A frozen box has no use-by until it is moved to the fridge (then 1 day).

Box 1 in the fridge. Boxes 2–4 in the freezer (keema and rice both freeze: 1 month for the rice, 3 months for the keema). Move the next day's box to the fridge the night before.


Chill everything within 60 minutes of cooking (spread on a tray). Cold boxes travel in an insulated bag with an ice pack and are eaten within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour above 32 °C).

## Eating and reheating

**Eat hot (microwave).** Microwave 3 min at full power with the lid ajar, stir, then 30–60 s more until steaming hot right through (75 °C). A spoon of water on the rice stops it drying. Reheat once only.

## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Pot for the eggs
- Soup bowl
- Can opener
- Knife and board

**Before you start**

- Boil the eggs (2 per dinner) 10 minutes, cool 5 minutes in cold water and keep them in the fridge: boiled eggs keep 3 days, so boil them no more than 3 days before you eat them.
- Keep the salmorejo brik in the fridge so it is very cold.

**Steps**

1. No cooking: only the eggs are boiled (up to 3 days ahead). Shake the brik and pour 250 g (about 1 cup) of cold salmorejo into the bowl. *(no heat)*
2. Peel and chop 2 boiled eggs and put them on top. *(no heat)*
3. Open 1 can of tuna, drain it (60 g) and add it on top. *(no heat)*
4. Add a few drops of olive oil (3 g, about ½ tsp). Eat with 30 g picos and an orange for dessert. *(no heat)*

**Box it up:** Close the opened brik and keep it in the fridge: it covers 2 dinners in a row (2 days once opened). Both portions go in the fridge: eat them within 2 days (by Tuesday if you make them on Sunday). Do not freeze. Keep a second, unopened brik in the cupboard. Boiled eggs keep 3 days.

**Reheat:** Not reheated: salmorejo is eaten very cold.

**Tips**

- Halal: the Hacendado salmorejo is tomato, bread, olive oil, pepper, garlic, salt and vinegar. No ham: add the egg and tuna instead of jamón.

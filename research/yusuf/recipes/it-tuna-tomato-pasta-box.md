## Method

1. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil 6 min. Add the tomato, oregano and chilli; simmer 10 min.
2. Add the peas for 4 min, then take off the heat and fold in the tuna and olives.
3. Boil the penne al dente, drain and toss with the sauce. Cool fast and box.

Use-by of a packed box = cooked date + the shortest fridge life in it = **1 day**. A frozen box has no use-by until it is moved to the fridge (then 1 day).

Box 1 in the fridge, boxes 2–4 in the freezer (1 month): move one down each evening. Or keep the sauce (3 days) apart and boil pasta the night before.


Chill everything within 60 minutes of cooking (spread on a tray). Cold boxes travel in an insulated bag with an ice pack and are eaten within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour above 32 °C).

## Eating and reheating

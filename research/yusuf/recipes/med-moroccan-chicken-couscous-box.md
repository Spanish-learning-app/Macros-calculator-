## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- 2 oven trays lined with baking paper
- Large bowl
- Heatproof bowl with a plate or lid (couscous)
- Kettle
- 4 lunch boxes

**Before you start**

- Cut the courgettes (400 g) into 2 cm chunks, about 5 medium carrots (300 g) into thick 1 cm slices, 2½ red peppers (300 g) into chunks and about 2 medium red onions (200 g) into wedges.
- Cut the chicken breast (680 g) into strips about 1.5 cm thick. Peel and grate 3 garlic cloves (10 g).
- Chop the parsley (15 g).

**Steps**

1. Turn the oven on to 220 °C. *(oven 220 °C · 10 min)*
2. In the big bowl toss the vegetables with 2 tbsp olive oil (25 g), 2 tsp ras el hanout (6 g), 1½ tsp ground cumin (3 g) and a heaped ½ tsp salt (4 g). Spread on the first tray. *(no heat)*
3. Roast the vegetables 15 minutes. *(oven 220 °C · 15 min)*
4. Meanwhile, in the same bowl, toss the chicken with 2½ tsp ras el hanout (8 g), the garlic, a little under 1 tbsp olive oil (12 g) and ¾ tsp salt (5 g). Spread on the second tray. *(no heat)*
5. Turn the vegetables over and put the chicken tray in the oven too. Roast both 15–18 minutes more. *(oven 220 °C · 18 min)*
6. Check: cut the thickest chicken strip. White all through with clear juices, and a knife slides into the carrots. Pink? 3–4 minutes more in the oven. Then squeeze 1½ tbsp lemon juice (20 g) over the chicken. *(no heat)*
7. Couscous: boil the kettle. Put the couscous (240 g), 1 tsp olive oil (5 g) and a heaped ¼ tsp salt (2 g) in the heatproof bowl, pour over 336 ml boiling water (a little under 1½ cups), stir and cover for 5 minutes. *(no heat · 5 min)*
8. Fluff the couscous with a fork and fold in the parsley. *(no heat)*
9. Spread everything out to cool fast (in the fridge or freezer within 1 hour). *(no heat · 30 min)*
10. Each box: a quarter of the couscous, vegetables and chicken. *(no heat)*

**Done when:** The chicken is white all through with clear juices (75 °C); the carrots are soft when you push in a knife.

**Box it up:** Cool fast, in the fridge or freezer within 1 hour. Couscous keeps only 1 day in the fridge. Box 1 in the fridge: eat it the next day. Boxes 2–4 in the freezer: eat within 1 month, and move one to the fridge the night before you eat it. Frozen, the couscous keeps 1 month, the chicken and vegetables 3 months.

**Reheat:** Hot: microwave with the lid loose 2½–3 minutes, stir, until steaming hot right through; reheat once only. Cold: eat within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour above 32 °C), carried with an ice pack; a squeeze of lemon helps.

**Tips**

- Ras el hanout blends vary: check the label for added salt and sugar.

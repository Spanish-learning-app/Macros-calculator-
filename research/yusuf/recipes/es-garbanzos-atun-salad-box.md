## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Small pot for 2 eggs
- Sieve and can opener
- Large bowl
- Chopping board and knife
- 2 lunch boxes

**Before you start**

- Dice about ¾ red pepper (100 g) and about ½ green pepper (50 g) into 1 cm pieces. Chop ½ medium red onion (50 g) small (3–5 mm).
- Halve the cherry tomatoes (160 g). Dice about ¾ medium cucumber (80 g) into 1 cm cubes. Chop the parsley (8 g, a small handful).

**Steps**

1. The only cooking is the eggs. Put 2 eggs in the pot, cover with cold water and bring to the boil. *(high heat · 5 min)*
2. Turn down so it bubbles gently and cook 10 minutes. *(medium heat · 10 min)*
3. Pour off the hot water, cover the eggs with cold water for 5 minutes, then peel them. *(no heat · 5 min)*
4. Check one egg: cut it in half. The yolk is firm and pale yellow all through. Soft in the middle? Boil the next ones 2 minutes longer. *(no heat)*
5. Drain and rinse the chickpeas (300 g, ¾ of a jar) and drain the tuna (220 g, about 4 cans). *(no heat)*
6. In the bowl mix the chickpeas, tuna, chopped vegetables and parsley. *(no heat)*
7. Dress with 2 tbsp olive oil (30 g), 1½ tbsp lemon juice (20 g), ½ tsp ground cumin (1 g, optional), a pinch of salt (1–2 g) and black pepper. Stir gently. *(no heat)*
8. Fill 2 boxes with half the salad each and put 1 halved egg on top of each. *(no heat)*

**Done when:** The egg yolks are firm and pale yellow all through.

**Box it up:** Cold only, no freezing. The dressed salad keeps 2 days and the boiled eggs 3 days, so make it 2 boxes at a time. Both boxes go in the fridge: eat them within 2 days (by Tuesday if you make them on Sunday). Do not freeze. For Wednesday and Thursday make it again on Tuesday night. Carry in an insulated bag with an ice pack.

**Reheat:** Not reheated (no fish or eggs in the microwave): eat cold within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour on a day above 32 °C).

**Tips**

- Lemon juice replaces wine vinegar. Tuna "al natural" (tuna, water, salt) has no oil to drain away, so the 60 g olive oil is all the fat.

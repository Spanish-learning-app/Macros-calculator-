## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Large microwave-safe bowl (about 2 l) with a microwave lid or a plate to cover it
- Microwave (times are for 800 W; add 30 seconds a step at 700 W)
- Oven glove (the bowl gets very hot)
- Sieve
- Grater
- 2 boxes and 2 small lidded pots (about 150 ml)

**Before you start**

- Chop ¾ medium onion (80 g) very fine.
- Peel and grate 2 garlic cloves (6 g) and a 2 cm piece of ginger (6 g) into a paste.
- Drain and rinse the chickpeas (1 jar, 400 g drained).
- Chop a few sprigs of coriander (6 g).

**Steps**

1. No cooking on the hob: everything is done in the microwave. Get the microwave bowl and its loose lid ready. *(no heat)*
2. In the bowl mix the onion, 2 tsp olive oil (10 g) and the ginger-garlic paste. Cover loosely and microwave until the onion is soft. *(microwave · 3 min)*
3. Stir in the powders: 1½ tsp garam masala (3 g), 1 tsp ground cumin (2 g), 1 tsp ground coriander (2 g), a heaped ¼ tsp red chilli powder (1 g), a heaped ¼ tsp haldi (turmeric powder, 1 g) and a heaped ¼ tsp salt (2 g). Microwave uncovered for only 30 seconds so the spices toast in the oil without burning. *(microwave)*
4. Add the crushed tomato (200 g, half a 400 g tin). Stir, cover and microwave 4 minutes, stirring halfway. *(microwave · 4 min)*
5. Add the chickpeas, stir, cover and microwave until bubbling. *(microwave · 3 min)*
6. Check: the sauce bubbles and is thick enough to coat the chickpeas, and the onion is soft, not crunchy. Thin? 1 more minute uncovered. *(no heat)*
7. Stir in 2 tsp lemon juice (10 g) and the chopped coriander. Split between the 2 boxes. *(no heat)*
8. Raita (no cooking): grate 60 g cucumber and squeeze it hard. Mix with 240 g protein yogurt and a few chopped mint leaves (2 g). Split into 2 pots. *(no heat · 2 min)*
9. To eat now: open the basmati cup a little at one corner (or as the pack says) and microwave 40 seconds. *(microwave)*

**Done when:** The sauce bubbles and is thick enough to coat the chickpeas; the onion is soft, not crunchy.

**Box it up:** Cool the second box on the counter (no longer than 1 hour). Eat portion 1 the day you make it. Portion 2 goes in the fridge: eat it within 3 days (by Wednesday if you make it on Sunday). It also freezes (3 months). The raita keeps 2 days and does not freeze; it takes 2 minutes to mix fresh. The rice cup stays sealed in the cupboard.

**Reheat:** Chana from the fridge: microwave covered 2–3 minutes, stirring halfway, until steaming hot all through. Once only. From frozen: move it to the fridge the night before. Rice cup: 40 seconds. Never microwave the raita or yogurt pot: it splits and goes watery. Eat it cold.

**Tips**

- Halal: chickpeas, tomato, spices and a protein yogurt of milk and cultures only.
- Spitting in the microwave? Keep the lid or plate on and stir with a long spoon.
- Too spicy? Leave out the chilli powder and eat it with more raita.
- Ginger-garlic paste: peel the garlic and ginger and grate both on the smallest holes of a grater, or blend them with 1 tsp water. A jar of ready paste from the Pakistani grocery is fine (use the same grams).

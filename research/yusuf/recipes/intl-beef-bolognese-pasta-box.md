## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Wide deep frying pan or casserole (28 cm) with a lid
- Wooden spoon
- Pot for 2 l of water and a colander
- Grater
- Oven tray or wide dish for cooling
- 4 lunch boxes or flat freezer bags

**Before you start**

- Peel about 1½ medium onions (150 g) and chop it small (5 mm).
- Peel and grate 2 medium carrots (120 g) on the big holes of the grater.
- Wipe the mushrooms (200 g) and chop them into 1 cm pieces.
- Peel 3 garlic cloves (9 g) and chop them finely.
- Open 1 jar (800 g) of crushed tomato.

**Steps**

1. Put the wide pan on the hob. Add a little under 1 tbsp olive oil (12 g) and heat 1 minute. *(medium-high heat · 1 min)*
2. Add the beef mince (500 g). Break it up with the wooden spoon and cook, stirring now and then, until there is no pink left and it starts to brown. *(medium-high heat · 5 min)*
3. Add the onion, carrot, mushrooms and garlic. Stir and cook until the onion is soft and see-through and the mushrooms have shrunk. *(medium heat · 8 min)*
4. Pour in the crushed tomato. Add 1 tsp dried oregano (2 g), 1 bay leaf, a heaped ½ tsp salt (4 g) and black pepper (1 g). Stir. *(medium heat)*
5. When it bubbles, turn the heat down so it bubbles gently. Put the lid on, leaving a small gap. Simmer 30–40 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes so it does not stick. *(low heat · 35 min)*
6. Check: drag a spoon across the pan. It leaves a clear line, and no meat is pink. Runny? Simmer 5–10 minutes more with the lid off. *(no heat)*
7. Take out the bay leaf. Spread the sauce on the tray or wide dish to cool fast (about 30 minutes). *(no heat · 30 min)*
8. Split the sauce into 4 equal portions (about 350 g each), in lunch boxes or in freezer bags pressed flat (Box it up says which go where). No pasta yet. *(no heat)*
9. The night before each lunch: boil 2 l of water with a pinch of salt. Add 80 g wholewheat penne (about 1 cup) for each box and cook for the time on the pack (about 10 minutes), stirring once. *(high heat · 10 min)*
10. Drain the penne in the colander, add it to the box with a portion of sauce and mix. Let it cool 20–30 minutes, then close and refrigerate. *(no heat · 30 min)*

**Done when:** The sauce is thick enough that a spoon dragged across the pan leaves a clear line, and no meat is pink.

**Box it up:** Cool fast, in the fridge within 1 hour. The sauce sets the dates: Boxes 1–3 in the fridge: eat them within 3 days (by Wednesday if you make them on Sunday). Box 4 in the freezer: eat within 3 months, and move one to the fridge the night before you eat it. Cooked penne keeps only 1 day, so boil it the night before each lunch and mix it with that box (or freeze plain cooked penne in portions for up to a month).

**Reheat:** Microwave with the lid loose 2½–3 minutes, stir, then 1 minute more, until steaming hot right through. Reheat once only. Frozen sauce: move it to the fridge the night before.

**Tips**

- No wine and no stock cube: the tomato and mushrooms give the depth.

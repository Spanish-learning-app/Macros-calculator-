## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Medium pot (3 l) with a lid
- Food processor (or a stick blender with a tall jug)
- Large non-stick frying pan
- Grater
- Plate with kitchen paper
- 4 lunch boxes and 4 small lidded pots (about 150 ml)

**Before you start**

- Roughly chop about 1 medium onion (100 g). Peel 5 garlic cloves (15 g, keep them whole) and a 3 cm piece of ginger (10 g).
- Whole spices: 1 tbsp coriander seeds (6 g), 1½ tsp zeera (cumin seeds, 3 g), 1 tsp black peppercorns (3 g), a small piece of cinnamon stick (1 g) and 2 cloves (½ g).
- For the mix: chop ½ medium red onion (60 g) very fine, 2 green chillies (10 g), a handful of coriander leaves (15 g) and of mint leaves (8 g).

**Steps**

1. Soak the chana dal (150 g, about ¾ cup) in cold water for 1 hour, then drain it. Do the rest of "Before you start" meanwhile. *(no heat · 60 min)*
2. Put the mince (500 g), drained chana dal, onion chunks, whole garlic, ginger, whole spices, 1½ tsp red chilli powder or chilli flakes (4 g) and 1 heaped tsp salt (7 g) in the pot with 600 ml water (about 2½ cups). Bring to the boil. *(high heat · 5 min)*
3. Lid on, simmer until the dal squashes easily between your fingers. *(low heat · 35 min)*
4. Lid off. Cook, stirring often, until it is completely dry: when you tilt the pot, no liquid runs and the mix starts to stick a little. This matters: a wet mix makes kebabs that break. *(medium-high heat · 10 min)*
5. Spread the mix on a plate to cool 15 minutes. Pick out the cinnamon stick and the cloves. *(no heat · 15 min)*
6. Blend it in the food processor (in 2 batches) into a smooth, dough-like paste. *(no heat · 3 min)*
7. Mix in the raw red onion, green chillies, coriander, mint and 1 egg with your hand. *(no heat)*
8. With wet hands shape 12 flat round patties (about 75 g each, 1 cm thick). Smooth any cracks on the edges. *(no heat · 6 min)*
9. Beat the second egg on a plate. Heat half the oil (12 g, a little under 1 tbsp) in the frying pan for 1 minute. *(medium heat · 1 min)*
10. Dip 6 patties in the egg, let the extra drip off, and fry 2 minutes a side until golden. Turn them once only, gently, with a flat spatula. Add the other 12 g oil and fry the other 6. *(medium heat · 8 min)*
11. Check one kebab: golden brown on both sides, it holds its shape when you lift it, and it is steaming hot in the middle when you cut it. Pale? 1 more minute a side. *(no heat)*
12. Let the kebabs cool on the plate with kitchen paper (20 minutes). *(no heat · 20 min)*
13. Salad (no cooking), half now: shred half the romaine (60 g), slice 1 tomato (100 g) and a few thin rings of red onion (20 g). Put the tomato and onion in the bottom of 2 boxes with 2 tsp lemon juice (10 g), lettuce on top. Make the other half on Tuesday night. *(no heat · 5 min)*
14. Mint-yogurt pots (no cooking), half now: grate 60 g cucumber and squeeze it hard. Mix with 160 g light Greek yogurt (about ¾ cup), half the chopped mint (4 g), a pinch of roasted ground cumin (½ g) and a pinch of salt (1 g). Split into 2 pots. Make 2 more on Tuesday night. *(no heat · 3 min)*
15. Each box: 3 kebabs next to the salad and 1 yogurt pot. The pita stays in its own bag; you fill it at lunch so it does not go soggy. *(no heat)*

**Done when:** Kebabs are golden brown on both sides, hold their shape when lifted and are hot in the middle.

**Box it up:** Cool and put away within 1 hour. Boxes 1–3 in the fridge: eat them within 3 days (by Wednesday if you make them on Sunday). Box 4 in the freezer: eat within 3 months, and move one to the fridge the night before you eat it. Salad and yogurt keep 2 days and cannot be frozen: make half on Sunday and half on Tuesday night. Pitas: keep the pack closed at room temperature, or freeze them.

**Reheat:** Good cold. To eat warm: microwave the 3 kebabs alone, covered, 60–90 seconds until hot all through, then build the pita. Never microwave the salad or the yogurt. Frozen kebabs: move them to the fridge the night before.

**Tips**

- Halal: beef from the halal butcher. The Mission pita has no E471; if you buy wraps instead, pick ones labelled "E471 de origen vegetal".
- Chana dal is split yellow chickpeas (Pakistani grocery). Do not swap in red lentils: they turn to mush and the kebabs fall apart.
- Kebabs breaking in the pan? The mix was too wet. Chill the shaped patties 30 minutes in the fridge before frying, and turn them only once.
- You can freeze the raw shaped patties between baking paper and fry them from frozen, 3 minutes a side.

## Method

1. Toss the chicken with oil, spices, garlic and salt; sear 6–8 min (75 °C inside).
2. In the same pan warm the beans and corn with the cumin, 3 min.
3. Box chicken, beans and corn with a sealed rice cup; salsa, yogurt and lettuce in a small box or pot.
4. At lunch: microwave the rice cup (1 min) and the chicken and beans until steaming; top with the cold salsa, yogurt and lettuce.

Use-by of a packed box = cooked date + the shortest fridge life in it = **2 days**. A frozen box has no use-by until it is moved to the fridge (then 1 day).

Boxes 1–3 in the fridge; box 4's chicken and beans in the freezer (3 months). Open the salsa jar on Sunday (it keeps 2 days opened): pot 2 boxes then and the rest on Tuesday night, or use the other half of the jar for the breakfast tacos.

- Sealed pack (microwave rice cup, can, single cheese tub): stays sealed in the box until you eat it; keeps to the date on the pack (rice cups and cans in the cupboard, Burgos tubs in the fridge). Once opened it follows the class of what it is mixed into.

Chill everything within 60 minutes of cooking (spread on a tray). Cold boxes travel in an insulated bag with an ice pack and are eaten within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour above 32 °C).

## Eating and reheating

**Eat hot or cold.** Chicken, beans and rice cup until steaming hot (75 °C). The salsa and yogurt stay cold.

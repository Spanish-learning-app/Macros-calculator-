## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Large deep pan or casserole (28 cm) with a lid
- Large bowl for the meat mix
- Wooden spoon
- Chopping board and knife
- 4 boxes

**Before you start**

- Peel and chop the onions (240 g, 2 medium) finely (3–5 mm).
- Peel about 3 medium potatoes (600 g) and cut them into 3 cm chunks. Keep them in a bowl of cold water.
- Peel and chop 2 garlic cloves (6 g) very finely. Chop the parsley (8 g).

**Steps**

1. In the bowl mix the beef mince (560 g), breadcrumbs (40 g, about 4 tbsp), 1 egg, the garlic, parsley, ¾ tsp salt (5 g) and black pepper (1 g) with your hands. *(no heat)*
2. Shape 20 meatballs of about 33 g (a little bigger than a walnut). Put them on a plate in the fridge. Wash your hands. *(no heat · 8 min)*
3. Sofregit: heat 2 tbsp + 1 tsp olive oil (32 g) in the casserole. Add the onions and cook slowly, stirring every few minutes, until soft and golden. *(low heat · 20 min)*
4. Stir in 1 tsp paprika (2 g) for a few seconds, then add half a jar of crushed tomato (400 g) and 1 bay leaf. Cook until thick. *(medium heat · 10 min)*
5. Add 300 ml water, the drained potatoes, a heaped ½ tsp salt (4 g) and the raw meatballs. Bring to a bubble. *(medium-high heat · 3 min)*
6. Put the lid on, turn down and simmer 20 minutes. Do not stir hard: shake the pan so the meatballs keep their shape. *(low heat · 20 min)*
7. Add the frozen peas (400 g (about 3 cups)). Simmer 10 minutes more with the lid a little open. *(low heat · 10 min)*
8. Check: a potato chunk breaks easily with a spoon, and a meatball cut in half has no pink inside. Pink or hard? Lid on, 5 minutes more. *(no heat)*
9. Take out the bay leaf and let it cool 30 minutes. *(no heat · 30 min)*
10. Fill 4 boxes: 5 meatballs, a quarter of the potatoes, peas and sauce each. *(no heat)*

**Done when:** A potato chunk breaks easily with a spoon and a meatball cut in half has no pink inside (75 °C).

**Box it up:** Cool fast, in the fridge or freezer within 1 hour. Portions 1–3 in the fridge: eat them within 3 days (by Wednesday if you make them on Sunday). Portion 4 in the freezer: eat within 3 months, and move one to the fridge the night before you eat it.

**Reheat:** Microwave covered 3–4 minutes, stirring halfway, until steaming hot all through (from frozen, defrost first). Or warm gently in a pan until it bubbles.

**Tips**

- Water, not stock: most shop stocks are chicken and not halal unless certified.
- Check the breadcrumbs label for manteca (lard).

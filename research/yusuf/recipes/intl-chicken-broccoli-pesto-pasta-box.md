## Method

1. Sear the seasoned chicken in the oil 6–8 min (75 °C inside).
2. Boil the penne; add the frozen broccoli for the last 3 minutes. Drain, keeping a cup of the water.
3. Blitz basil, almonds, oil, garlic, lemon and salt with 40 g of the pasta water.
4. Toss pasta, broccoli, chicken and pesto; cool fast. Add the tomatoes when you box it.

Use-by of a packed box = cooked date + the shortest fridge life in it = **1 day**. A frozen box has no use-by until it is moved to the fridge (then 1 day).

Pasta keeps 1 day in the fridge, so box 1 goes in the fridge and boxes 2–4 in the freezer (1 month); move one down each evening. Freeze without the tomatoes and add a handful in the morning.


Chill everything within 60 minutes of cooking (spread on a tray). Cold boxes travel in an insulated bag with an ice pack and are eaten within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour above 32 °C).

## Eating and reheating

**Eat hot or cold.** Hot: microwave until steaming (75 °C), stir once; or eat cold within 2 h of leaving the fridge.

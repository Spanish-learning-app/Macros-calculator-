## Method

1. Toss the chicken with paprika, garlic, salt and pepper; sear in the oil 6–8 min (75 °C inside). Cool and slice.
2. Boil the penne al dente (about 11 min), rinse under cold water, drain well.
3. Whisk the hummus with lemon juice and water.
4. Toss pasta with the dressing, then fold in the vegetables and chicken. Each box gets a quarter.

Use-by of a packed box = cooked date + the shortest fridge life in it = **1 day**. A frozen box has no use-by until it is moved to the fridge (then 1 day).

Cooked pasta keeps 1 day in the fridge and the dressed vegetables 2 days, so: box 1 assembled on Sunday; pasta for boxes 2–4 frozen plain (1 month) and chicken frozen or chilled (3 days). Each evening move one pasta and one chicken portion to the fridge and toss with dressing and vegetables in the morning (2 minutes). Cut the vegetables on Sunday and again on Tuesday.


Chill everything within 60 minutes of cooking (spread on a tray). Cold boxes travel in an insulated bag with an ice pack and are eaten within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour above 32 °C).

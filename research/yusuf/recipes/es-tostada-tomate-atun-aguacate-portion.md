## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Box grater
- Small lidded pot (about 250 ml)
- Small pot for the eggs (if you have no boiled eggs)
- Toaster or a dry frying pan
- Sieve

**Before you start**

- The night before: cut the tomatoes (200 g, 2 medium) in half. Grate the cut side on the big holes of the grater until only the skin is left in your hand. Throw away the skin.
- Stir a heaped ¼ tsp salt (1 g) into the tomato pulp and keep it in the small pot in the fridge (enough for 2 mornings).
- If you have no eggs boiled in the last 3 days, boil 2 eggs (10 minutes in bubbling water, 5 in cold water) and peel them.

**Steps**

1. No cooking: only the bread is toasted and the eggs are boiled. In the morning cut ½ roll (65 g) open. *(no heat)*
2. Toast it in the toaster, or 2 minutes a side in a dry pan, until golden and crisp at the edges. *(medium heat · 3 min)*
3. Spoon half the tomato pulp onto the toast. Drizzle 1 tsp olive oil (3 g). *(no heat)*
4. Open 1 can of tuna, drain it (60 g) and put it on top. *(no heat)*
5. Slice ¼ avocado (50 g) and 1 boiled egg and lay them on top. *(no heat)*

**Done when:** The bread is golden and crisp at the edges.

**Box it up:** Both portions go in the fridge: eat them within 2 days (by Tuesday if you make them on Sunday). Do not freeze. The tomato pulp keeps 2 days in the fridge; boiled eggs 3 days. Wrap the unused half avocado with its stone in and a drop of lemon, and keep it in the fridge for the next morning. Freeze the rest of the rolls if you will not use them in 3 days.

**Reheat:** Not reheated: eaten as soon as it is made.

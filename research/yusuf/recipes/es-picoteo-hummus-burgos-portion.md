## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Pot for the Sunday eggs
- Chopping board and knife
- 2 boxes and 2 small lidded pots (about 100 ml)
- A plate to serve

**Before you start**

- Boil the eggs (2 per portion) 10 minutes, cool 5 minutes in cold water and keep them in the fridge: boiled eggs keep 3 days, so boil them no more than 3 days before you eat them.

**Steps**

1. No cooking: only the eggs are boiled (up to 3 days ahead). Peel about 2½ medium carrots (160 g) and cut them into sticks 1 cm thick. Cut the red pepper (140 g) into strips and the cucumber (160 g) into sticks. *(no heat)*
2. Split the sticks and cherry tomatoes (120 g) between the 2 boxes. *(no heat)*
3. Spoon 70 g hummus (about 3½ tbsp) into each small pot. *(no heat)*
4. Add 2 peeled boiled eggs and 2 sealed Burgos tubs to each box. *(no heat)*
5. To eat: tip the 2 Burgos tubs onto a plate, drizzle ½ tsp olive oil (3 g) and a pinch of oregano. Dip the vegetables and 30 g picos in the hummus. *(no heat)*

**Box it up:** Straight into the fridge. Both portions go in the fridge: eat them within 2 days (by Tuesday if you make them on Sunday). Do not freeze. Cut vegetables and opened hummus keep 2 days: make 2 boxes at a time. Picos stay in their bag; Burgos tubs stay sealed until you eat.

**Reheat:** Not reheated: everything is eaten cold.

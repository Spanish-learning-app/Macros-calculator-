## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Wide deep frying pan (28 cm)
- Small frying pan (20 cm) with a lid, for the night
- Wooden spoon
- 4 freezer boxes or bags and 4 small lidded pots

**Before you start**

- Slice about 2 medium onions (200 g) thin. Cut 2½ red peppers (300 g) into strips 1 cm wide.
- Peel and chop 3 garlic cloves (9 g).
- Drain the chickpeas (1 jar, 400 g). Open 1 jar (800 g) of crushed tomato.

**Steps**

1. Heat 1½ tbsp olive oil (20 g) in the wide pan for 1 minute. *(medium heat · 1 min)*
2. Add the onion and peppers. Cook, stirring now and then, until soft and floppy. *(medium heat · 8 min)*
3. Add the garlic, 2 tsp ground cumin (4 g), 1 tbsp paprika (6 g) and a pinch of chilli powder (1 g). Stir 1 minute. *(medium heat · 1 min)*
4. Pour in the crushed tomato, add the chickpeas and ½ tsp salt (3 g). Stir. *(medium heat)*
5. When it bubbles, turn down and simmer 15 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick. *(low heat · 15 min)*
6. Check: the sauce is thick (a spoon dragged across leaves a line), the peppers are soft and it bubbles all over. Runny? 5 more minutes. *(no heat)*
7. Cool for 20–30 minutes. *(no heat · 25 min)*
8. Split into 4 portions (about 360 g each), in boxes or flat freezer bags. *(no heat)*
9. On the night: put one portion of sauce in the small pan and heat it until it bubbles. *(medium heat · 4 min)*
10. Make 2 hollows in the sauce with a spoon. Crack 1 egg into each hollow. *(medium heat)*
11. Put the lid on and cook 6–8 minutes, until the egg whites are fully white and firm. *(low heat · 7 min)*
12. Serve with 60 g Greek yogurt (3 tbsp) and ½ roll (65 g). *(no heat)*

**Done when:** The egg whites are fully white and firm; the yolks are as runny or as set as you like (cook 2 minutes more for a firm yolk).

**Box it up:** Cool the sauce for no more than 1 hour. Portions 1–3 in the fridge: eat them within 3 days (by Wednesday if you make them on Sunday). Portion 4 in the freezer: eat within 3 months, and move one to the fridge the night before you eat it. The eggs are cracked in fresh on the night (cooked eggs are not frozen) and the yogurt is spooned out on the night. Freeze the rolls if you will not use them within 3 days.

**Reheat:** The sauce is heated in the pan until it bubbles before the eggs go in (step 8). From the freezer: move a portion to the fridge the night before.

**Tips**

- Eat it straight away once the eggs are in: do not box and reheat a cooked shakshuka egg.

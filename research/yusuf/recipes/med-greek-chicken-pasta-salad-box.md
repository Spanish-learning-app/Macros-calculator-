## Method

This is the method the app shows under "How to make it" (generated from the same data).

**Equipment**

- Frying pan (28 cm)
- Large pot for 3 l of water and a colander
- Bowl for the dressing
- Chopping board and knife
- 4 lunch boxes, 4 small lidded pots (about 100 ml) and 3 freezer bags

**Before you start**

- If the chicken breasts (540 g) are thicker than 1.5 cm, cut them through the middle into thin fillets.
- Peel 4 garlic cloves: grate 2 (6 g) for the chicken and 2 (6 g) for the dressing.
- Dice the cucumber (300 g) and green pepper (160 g) into 1 cm pieces. Halve the cherry tomatoes (320 g). Slice the red onion (60 g) thin. Halve the olives (80 g).
- Chop the mint leaves (6 g) finely.

**Steps**

1. Rub the chicken with 1½ tsp dried oregano (3 g), the 6 g grated garlic, 2 tsp lemon juice (10 g), ½ tsp salt (3 g) and black pepper (1 g). *(no heat)*
2. Heat 2 tsp olive oil (8 g) in the frying pan for 1 minute. *(medium-high heat · 1 min)*
3. Add the chicken. Cook 3–4 minutes without moving, until golden underneath. *(medium-high heat · 4 min)*
4. Turn and cook 3–4 minutes more. *(medium-high heat · 4 min)*
5. Check: cut the thickest piece. White all through, no pink, juices clear. Pink? 1–2 minutes more a side. Then cool it on a plate and slice it. *(no heat)*
6. Boil 3 l of water with a pinch of salt. Add the wholewheat penne (280 g, about 3½ cups) and cook for the pack time (about 10 minutes). *(high heat · 10 min)*
7. Drain the penne and rinse it under the cold tap until it is cold. Shake it dry in the colander. *(no heat)*
8. Make the dressing: mix 240 g Greek yogurt, 1¾ tbsp olive oil (24 g), 2 tbsp lemon juice (30 g), the 6 g grated garlic, the mint and a heaped ¼ tsp salt (2 g). Spoon into 4 pots (about 77 g each). *(no heat)*
9. Tomorrow's box: a quarter of the pasta (about 160 g), a quarter of the chicken and a quarter of the vegetables. Dressing pot next to it. *(no heat)*
10. Put each other pasta portion with a quarter of the chicken in a freezer bag and freeze it. Keep the cut vegetables in a closed box in the fridge. *(no heat)*
11. At lunch: pour the dressing over and toss. *(no heat)*

**Done when:** Chicken: white all through, no pink, juices clear (75 °C). Pasta: soft with a little bite.

**Box it up:** Cool fast, everything in the fridge or freezer within 1 hour. Cooked pasta keeps only 1 day in the fridge, so only tomorrow's box is assembled; the others freeze as pasta and chicken. Box 1 in the fridge: eat it the next day. Boxes 2–4 in the freezer: eat within 1 month, and move one to the fridge the night before you eat it. In the morning add vegetables and a dressing pot. Cut vegetables and dressing keep 2 days and do not freeze: cut a fresh batch on Tuesday night.

**Reheat:** Not reheated: eat cold within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour on a day above 32 °C).

**Tips**

- No feta in this salad, so no rennet to check. Dill is not sold at Mercadona: mint works.

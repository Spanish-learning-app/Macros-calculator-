# Report to the Boss #1 — Sprint 1 complete

**From:** Omar Farooq (Manager) · **Date:** 2026-10-14

## Short version

Katori's first full version is live: https://claude.ai/artifact/GusJdyjFkPK4yftNRPzFrY
It logs a full Pakistani day in about 23 taps (15 seconds), knows 184 foods with a source behind
each number, runs on Madrid time, and is honest about halal. Three testers scored it 7–7.5/10, and
every fix they asked for in Review 1 is in this version.

## What you can do in it today

- Start with no account. Skip setup or enter your details (feet/inches works). Targets come from a
  dietitian-designed formula with safety limits, always labelled as an estimate.
- **Day view:** a big "kcal left" and "protein left", and meals named for your schedule: Breakfast /
  café, Lunch, Merienda, Dinner, Snacks (Pakistani names are one tap away).
- **Add food:** search in Roman Urdu, Urdu, Spanish, Catalan or English (chaye, saalan, anday,
  pa amb tomàquet). "+" logs your usual portion in one tap. Portions are tapped, not typed: ½, 1,
  1½, katori sizes, "thora sa" for tasting. Home, restaurant and dhaba versions of oily dishes are
  one switch apart.
- **12 halal lunch boxes**, each loggable as "1 box". **Halal status** on every food, with the
  reason. Pastries that may contain lard say so.
- Week view, copy a meal or a whole day, undo, move to another day, export and import your data.

## The data

184 foods: Pakistani home, restaurant and street dishes, breads and rice, chai variants, sweets,
Barcelona café and bakery items, Spanish dishes, gym staples, packaged Pakistani foods, and your
lunch boxes. Dr. Ayesha reviewed 131 records: 109 passed, 19 were fixed, and 6 need more work in
Sprint 2. None were rejected. Examples of what review caught: plain naan was 20% too heavy (dough
weight, not cooked), daal mash in a katori was overcounted, and keema came down from 472 to 401 kcal
a katori.

## Problems and how we handled them

- **The usage limit stopped the team twice.** The hourly check-in restarted everyone automatically.
  The second stop was my mistake: I restarted the team in a way that used up the budget in minutes.
  The playbook now says: restart with fresh, small workers, at most three at a time.

## Next: Sprint 2 (meal prep)

Cook once, split into boxes or jars, then put one into any meal with one tap. Each box is marked
fridge or freezer, with use-by dates (cooked rice keeps only 1 day in the fridge). You get a shopping
list you can copy, whole-pot recipes, a personal chai, saved meals, and breakfast and dinner preps
on top of the 12 lunch boxes.

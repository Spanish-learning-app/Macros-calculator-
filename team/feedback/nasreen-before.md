# Nasreen Akhtar: "before" interview

*2026-10-13 · with Bilal. I haven't seen the app yet. I have no scale, so there are no
measurements here, just how I eat and think.*

## 1. My day

We are five: my husband, our daughter Hira and two sons. I make nashta in the morning and the main
pot in the afternoon: salan, daal or sabzi, chawal some days. Lunch is last night's salan with
fresh roti.

What I eat:
- **Morning:** chai, then the half paratha the children left, or one roti with last night's salan.
  Some days nothing till eleven.
- **While cooking:** I taste for salt with a chamach a few times and pick a piece of aloo out of
  the pot. I never think of this as eating.
- **Afternoon:** one roti with whatever is left, or some rice from the pot.
- **Shaam:** chai with two biscuits or a rusk; pakoray or a samosa if a neighbour comes.
- **Dinner:** one roti, sometimes one and a half, with salan, a little raita and salad. Then
  whatever rice and half roti the children left. I don't throw roti away.
- **Chai:** three or four cups, doodh patti with cheeni. I don't know how much my cup holds.

## 2. Portions

- **Roti:** I trust this one most. But ask me "ghar ki ya tandoor ki?"
- **Katori:** I have three sizes. If it just says "1 katori", I'll pick wrong. Show small, medium
  and large with a drawing. I don't know how many grams mine holds.
- **Plate:** "half plate chawal" makes sense, but show a picture here too.
- **Chamach:** this one confuses me. It's my small cheeni spoon *and* my big serving spoon. Call
  the big one "karchi".
- **Mutthi:** only for raw daal or chanay, never for cooked food.
- **Thora sa:** no app can turn "a little rice" into a true number. Still, give me a "small"
  button, or I'll skip it and the day will look better than it was.
- **Please no** grams first, oz, "1 serving", "cup" (which cup?) or 0.5. Say *aadhi* and
  *dairh*, or big ½ and 1½ buttons, with the grams small underneath for Hira or Bilal.

## 3. Cooking for the family

One pot for five, leftovers tomorrow. I don't know how many katoris come out of it. The only
weight I know is what the shopkeeper weighed. The oil goes in by andaza, and I use more than the
TV doctors say.

How I want to log: find "chicken salan, ghar ka", tap "1 katori", done. The next day, "kal wala
salan" in one tap. If you add a pot option later, ask about the oil as *kam / normal / zyada*.

I'd give up if it asks for the weight of the pot or the oil in ml, makes me divide by five, or
makes me type the same salan every day.

## 4. Fears and turn-offs

- **English words.** What does "macros" mean? Is kcal the same as calories? Explain in one simple
  line.
- **Too many numbers.** Decimals everywhere look like a bank statement. Show one big number: what's
  left today.
- **Guilt.** If I see red, sad faces or "You went over!", I'll close the app for good. I'll eat
  more at a shaadi or on Eid. Tell me calmly and let tomorrow start fresh.
- **Sign-up, OTP, password.** No. Say plainly that my data stays on my phone. If a week of it
  disappears, I won't start again.
- **Small text and buttons.** I hold the phone in one hand, and the other has atta on it.
- **Questions I can't answer.** I know my height in feet and inches, not cm. "Sedentary"? I'm on
  my feet all day but I don't exercise. Use examples like "housework, no exercise".
- **Hira setting it up.** She'd be quick and impatient and I'd feel stupid. I want to do it myself.

## 5. The first five minutes

Tell me in one line: "Write what you eat. See how much is left today." No account, one big button.
Then a few simple questions about me, with "skip". Then my number, labelled "this is a guess, you
can change it". Then straight to "What did you have today?" with chai, roti, paratha and anda at
the top. Let me set up my chai once, cheeni included, then one tap.

Search must understand Roman Urdu the way I type on WhatsApp: "chaye", "saalan", "anday",
"chawal", and my spelling mistakes too.

Questions I'd be too shy to ask:
- Do I count the tasting? The children's leftovers?
- I forgot lunch. Is the whole day spoiled? Can I fill it in at night?
- My achar or my own sabzi isn't there. Now what?
- What does "estimate" mean?
- How do I go back? Will the phone's back button close everything?

## 6. Success after two weeks

I write most days in a minute or two, without Hira after the first day. I can see what makes my
number big: cheeni, oil, the extra roti, the leftovers. The week looks "mostly okay", not red. I
trust that "1 katori salan" is close enough, and I know whether I eat enough protein, with desi
ideas, not lectures. The app feels like it's on my side.

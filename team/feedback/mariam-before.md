# Mariam Iqbal: "before" interview

*2026-10-13, late (already the 14th in Barcelona) · with Bilal. I haven't seen Katori yet. I don't
weigh food, so there are no gram weights here. Where I don't know a number, I say so.*

## 1. A PhD weekday (Madrid time)

- **08:30, café near campus.** Café con leche and a croissant at the bar. Every time I wonder:
  butter or lard? I never ask. At 11:00, a cortado with labmates. Still breakfast, not a snack.
- **13:30, lunch box.** Two microwaves for the floor, and a queue. On bad days I eat cold or at
  14:30. My portion is "one box". I don't know what it weighs.
- **17:30, merienda.** Lab chai: teabag, milk, one sugar sachet. Fruit or biscuits with it.
- **19:00, gym** three days a week. I want more protein, so a skyr after.
- **21:30, dinner.** Desi if there's keema left, or anda bhurji with toast. Otherwise quick: pasta
  with tuna, or a Mercadona tortilla with salad. Some nights, toast and milk at 00:30 over papers.

My portions are "one box", "my mug of chai", "half a tortilla". Never grams.

## 2. Sunday meal prep

On Saturday I go to the halal butcher in the Raval. On Sunday I make a big pot of rice, chicken
tikka (thighs) in the oven, keema matar, and some weeks shami kebabs. That's four boxes for Monday
to Thursday; Friday is the menú del día with the lab. I don't know the grams. Spare kebabs go in
the freezer, but I get one shelf, shared with two flatmates.

What goes wrong:
- **Thursday:** tikka again, so I buy a sandwich and the box dies.
- **Rice:** hard from the fridge, soggy from the microwave, and raita leaks onto it.
- **The forgotten box:** found on Saturday at the back of the fridge. Which Sunday? Bin.
- **The ice brick:** I forgot to move the box out of the freezer, so at 13:30 it was frozen.

What I want Katori to remember: what I cooked and when, boxes left and where (fridge or freezer),
a quiet evening line ("tomorrow's box is still in the freezer"), and last week's recipe, so I can
repeat it in a few taps or notice it's my third tikka week.

## 3. Halal life

- **Butcher:** certificate on the wall; my whole community buys there. The only meat I cook.
- **Mercadona:** I check *manteca* (lard, not *mantequilla*), *gelatina*, "grasa animal" and E471.
  "Sin cerdo" isn't enough. Wraps and biscuits surprise me most.
- **Bakeries and cafés:** "croissant de mantequilla" on the sign and I relax; just "croissant" and
  I wonder. Ensaïmada is made with lard. Bocadillos are mostly jamón, so I order tortilla or atún.
- **Menú del día:** lentejas usually come with chorizo and the chicken is almost never halal, so
  I take salad and grilled fish. I eat fish and prawns, not calamares. My Moroccan labmate eats all seafood. Don't
  choose for us. Ask.

**I'd trust** a `check` with one plain reason ("may be made with lard, ask the bakery") over a
confident green tick, and being told when the filter hid something. **I'd distrust** a green
"halal" on a café croissant or restaurant chicken, a ruling on prawns, or preaching.

## 4. MyFitnessPal and Yazio

- **MyFitnessPal:** dozens of user-made "chicken karahi" entries with different numbers, plus ads.
  My 00:30 toast landed on the next day. Splitting a Sunday pot into four boxes was so slow that I
  quit in week two.
- **Yazio:** prettier, but it pushed PRO and fasting plans and wanted an account. "Keema" found
  nothing; "lentejas" found the chorizo stew. A red over-budget bar after a Friday menú finished it.

## 5. Habit or delete

**Habit:** in the microwave queue, my box is one tap because I planned it on Sunday. Café
breakfast is "my usual" in one tap; chai is set up once. The day runs on Madrid time, and 00:30
still belongs to tonight. Search understands Catalan, Spanish without accents, and English. After
the gym I see protein left without digging.

**Delete:** sign-up or ads, lost data, a halal tick on something with lard, red guilt, weighing,
or calling my 21:30 dinner a "late-night snack".

# Project Brief from the Boss

**Product:** an interactive daily meal and macro calculator, built around a daily meal calendar.

**Who it is for first:** the Boss. They live in Pakistan, eat Pakistani food every day, and also eat
mixed international food (Spanish, Mediterranean, Western staples). They track calories, protein,
carbs, fat and fiber.

**What the Boss asked for, in their words (condensed):**

- A dashboard or app for a daily meal calendar where you plan and log what you eat and see your macros.
- It must know Pakistani food properly (roti, daal, salan, biryani, karahi, chai and so on) and
  international food too.
- It must be the best version the team can make. Take as long as needed.
- The team works on its own, holds meetings when it decides to, hires people when it needs them,
  and reports back to the Boss when there is something worth reporting.

**Quality bar:**

- Nutrition numbers must be defensible: every food carries a source note.
- Works on a phone first (the Boss will open it on a phone), and on desktop.
- No sign-up, no ads, no paywall. Data stays on the Boss's device.
- Fast to log a normal Pakistani day (for example: anda paratha + chai for nashta, daal chawal for
  lunch, chai + biscuits in the evening, chicken karahi + 2 roti for dinner) in under a minute.

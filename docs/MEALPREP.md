# Halal lunch-box library

**Owner:** Yusuf Benali (Nutrition Lab) · **Reviewed by:** Dr. Ayesha Siddiqui (pending) · **Prices and labels read:** 2026-10-13

Twelve halal lunch boxes you can batch-cook on Sunday and carry to university. Each one makes **4 boxes**, has 35–50 g of protein and 550–750 kcal, and most have at least 8 g of fibre. In the app, search for the box and log **"1 box"** (or "½ box").

Five are hot boxes for the microwave, three work hot or cold, and four are cold-only for days when the microwave queue is long. Meat comes from a halal butcher; everything else is on the Mercadona list in each recipe.

Every number comes from a standard recipe calculation (NUTRITION.md §6). Each recipe sheet shows the ingredients in grams, the sources, the yields, storage per component, a shopping list and a cost per box.

## A Sunday that works

- **Pick two boxes a week**, one hot and one cold, so Thursday is not the same as Monday.
- **Rice, pasta, couscous and bulgur keep 1 day in the fridge.** In a rice or couscous batch, box 1 goes in the fridge and boxes 2–4 go in the freezer. Move tomorrow's box to the fridge the night before.
- **Cooked chicken, meat and kebabs keep 3 days**, tortilla 3 days (do not freeze it), and cut salads, raita and yogurt pots 2 days. Salad boxes are mixed twice: Sunday for Monday and Tuesday, Tuesday night for Wednesday and Thursday.
- **Cool fast:** spread food on a tray and get it in the fridge within an hour.
- **Reheat once,** until steaming hot right through (75 °C). Sauce, raita and yogurt stay in their own pot and are never microwaved. No fish or egg boxes go in the microwave.
- **Cold boxes** travel in an insulated bag with an ice pack. Eat them within 2 hours of leaving the fridge (1 hour on a day above 32 °C).
- In the app, a box's use-by is the cooked date plus the shortest fridge life in it. A frozen box has no use-by until you move it to the fridge.

## Halal shopping in Barcelona

**Meat:** buy chicken and beef from a halal butcher (*carnicería halal*): there are several in the Raval, Sant Antoni and Poble Sec, and online halal butchers deliver in the province. Supermarket chicken and *caldo de pollo* (stock) are not halal unless the pack carries a halal certification seal.

**Labels:** read the ingredients, not the front of the pack.

- *Manteca* or *manteca de cerdo* is lard. *Mantequilla* is butter (fine). *Manteca de cacao* is cocoa butter (fine).
- *Gelatina* is usually pork gelatine unless it says *bovina halal* or *vegetal*. Check yogurts, desserts, sweets and "light" dairy.
- *E471* and *E472* (mono- and diglycerides) can come from animal fat. Accept them only when the label says *de origen vegetal*. On 2026-10-13 every wheat wrap at Mercadona Barcelona listed E471 with no origin, which is why the shami box uses Mission pita (no E471). Hacendado hummus marks its additives as *de origen vegetal*.
- *Sebo* is tallow. *Jamón, chorizo, fuet, sobrasada, panceta, butifarra* are pork. *Sin cerdo* ("no pork") is not the same as halal.
- Wine hides in sauces, stocks and stews (*al vino*, *con vino blanco*, *jerez*, *coñac*). These recipes use lemon or water instead.

**Bakeries and cafés:** a croissant *de mantequilla* is made with butter. Cheap pastries and some *bollería* are made with lard, and an *ensaïmada* is made with pork lard (*saïm*) by definition. When in doubt, ask "¿lleva manteca de cerdo?" (Catalan: "porta saïm?"). *Pa amb tomàquet* and *café con leche* are fine; most bocadillos are pork, so pick *tortilla* or *atún*.

**Cross-contact is not tracked.** Katori marks each food halal, check or not halal from its ingredients. It does not track shared slicers, grills, fryers or bread boards in cafés, bakeries and butchers. If that matters to you, ask.

## Planned for Sprint 2: breakfast and dinner preps

The Boss asked for meal prep at every meal, not just lunch (2026-10-13). Sprint 2 adds two more sets, built the same way: standard batch recipe, per-portion macros, storage per component, shopping list and cost. Serving names follow the container: "1 box", "1 jar", "1 portion". If the Lab widens the `lunchbox` group into a general meal-prep group, these records follow `rules.js`.

**Breakfast (5–6 preps, 25–40 g protein each, cold or reheated in 1–2 minutes):**

1. Overnight oats with skyr-style protein yogurt (or whey), fruit and nuts: "1 jar", 4–5 days.
2. Egg-and-vegetable muffins (whole eggs and egg whites, peppers, spinach, cheese): "1 portion" of 3 muffins, 3 days in the fridge or frozen.
3. Chia pudding with milk and protein yogurt: "1 jar".
4. Protein pancakes (oats, eggs, skyr) to freeze and toast: "1 portion".
5. Freezer anda-paratha rolls and aloo parathas: "1 roll", from frozen to the pan or microwave.
6. Halal breakfast wraps (egg, halal turkey or chicken, cheese) in a wrap without animal E471: "1 wrap".

**Dinner (6–8 freezer-friendly preps, eaten at home, so daal and gravies are welcome):** built with Zainab Rauf (Pakistani cuisine researcher), reusing her home-style standard recipes where they exist.

1. Chicken karahi base, frozen flat in portions.
2. Keema (plain or matar) in portions.
3. Chicken jalfrezi.
4. Aloo gosht with halal beef or mutton.
5. Daal (masoor or chana) in portions.
6. Seekh and shami kebabs, frozen raw or cooked, to grill on the night.
7. Roti and paratha stock: frozen, separated with baking paper.
8. One Spanish dinner, for example halal-beef *mandonguilles* with peas or chicken *escudella*-style soup.

For each one Sprint 2 adds: portions per batch, "1 portion" macros, freezer life, how to reheat from frozen, and a shared weekly shopping list across breakfast, lunch and dinner.

## Where the numbers come from

- Ingredient values: USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy and Foundation), IFCT 2017 (chana dal), and product labels read on 2026-10-13 (Open Food Facts entries checked against the Mercadona product page).
- Cooking yields: Bognár A. (2002), *Tables on weight yield of food and retention factors of food constituents*, and water balances from USDA raw and cooked pairs.
- Storage: `RULES.mealPrep.storage` (NHS and UK FSA for rice; USDA FSIS for cooked meat and egg dishes; a conservative Lab rule for salads).
- Prices: tienda.mercadona.es (Barcelona, postal code 08001) and carnehalalonline.com, both read 2026-10-13. Prices change: the date is part of the number.
- Calculation: `node research/yusuf/fetch.mjs`, then `node research/yusuf/build.mjs`. This page, the 12 recipe sheets and `app/data/foods-mealprep.js` are generated from the same run.
